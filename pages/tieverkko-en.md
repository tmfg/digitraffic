## General

<!-- TODO (Road Network team): description of this section -->

This page describes the structure of the Finnish road network and its
related datasets. Road network information forms the basis for many other
road traffic datasets provided by Digitraffic, such as traffic messages and
weather data, which are located at various points along the road network.

## Datasets

<!-- TODO: links/descriptions of relevant APIs and datasets -->

Road network datasets are published through Digitraffic APIs in a
machine-readable format. The datasets include, among other things, the
geometry of the road network, road numbers and addresses, and other
information related to the structure of the road network, which can be
utilized, for example, in routing and mapping applications.

More information about the road network datasets and their use can be found
