---
title: Park & ride information service LIIPI will be shut down during 2026
lang: en
ref: tie-liipi-suljetaan-vuonna-2026
traffictypes:
  - Road traffic
status: planned
date: 2026-08-28
---

The park & ride information service LIIPI will be shut down during 2026. The data of the parking facilities will be
transferred to the new Stop and Accessibility database https://peti.fintraffic.fi/ui/. The real-time availability
status of the parking facilities will no longer be handled in PETI. Instead, the real-time data must be retrieved
directly from the interfaces provided by the operators.
