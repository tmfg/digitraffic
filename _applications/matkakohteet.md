Matkakohteet.fi helps travellers plan domestic trips in Finland: destinations, about 45,000 places with opening hours, events, current weather and snow conditions, and how to get there. Digitraffic data is used for train station locations (rata.digitraffic.fi passenger stops) on destination and place pages, and for road weather camera images (tie.digitraffic.fi weathercam) showing current road and weather conditions near destinations.
