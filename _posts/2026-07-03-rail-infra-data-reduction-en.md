The Finnish Transport Infrastructure Agency has decided to restrict the distribution of certain railway network infrastructure data as open data. These data types, now classified as non-public, will be removed from Digitraffic by the end of 2026.

## Data types being fully removed

- Axle counters
- Balises
- Separators
- Separation sections
- Separation fields
- Hotbox detectors
- Reverse buttons
- Turntables
- Heating stations
- Groundings
- Signals
- Local point operation groups
- Stopping devices
- Wheel force indicators
- Rail insulations
- Derailers
- RFID readers
- Section insulators
- Electrical work insulators
- Switch heating transformers
- Pantograph monitoring cameras

## Data types with reduced content

**Switches:**
- Information about electric switch operation will be removed
- Information about permitted speed on diverging track will be removed

**Tracks:**
- Information about track inflection points (vertical geometry) will be removed
- Information about track usage purpose will be removed
